Now: the line at the bottom left says what to do next.

Hearts are your strength. Long roads and farm work use them up; resting on your porch and eating bring them back. At none, rest.

Screens count the distance from town on the road, one screen at a time.

Portals (sage green, three gold dots) step you between places at no cost in hearts.

Days end with the town paper, which prints what you did. The game saves when you sleep and when you rest.

Text & source

The game quotes the chapter “Pioneers” in Gabrielle Zevin’s Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow. Day 1 (L01–L29) follows the supplied adaptation of the chapter’s opening, quoted exactly. Later days (L2.01 and on) quote the chapter word for word; where a line drops a speech tag (“Alabaster said”), the comma before it becomes a full stop. The short connective lines under “Written for the game” are not from the book. All pixel art is hand-authored; no generated images are used.
